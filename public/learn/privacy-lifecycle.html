Privacy and the data life cycle

What personal data exists, where it is born, how long it lives, and every way it dies. Includes Atlassian's Personal Data Reporting API, which the Cloud edition implements.

Life cycle

Ways data is erased

  • Delete account (you): every row for you, immediately.
  • Disconnect Jira (you): tokens, sites, monitor state, devices and sessions immediately; the rest 30 days after your last sign-in unless you reconnect.
  • Revocation (you at Atlassian, or expiry): grant marked revoked; everything erased 30 days after your last sign-in.
  • Atlassian says "closed": that person's account erased, and their name replaced with "Former user" in everyone's history.
  • Atlassian says "updated": stale copy cleared (history names dropped; your profile fields cleared until next sign-in).
  • Age: history older than 30 days, expired sessions and rate-limit rows, every hour.

The Personal Data Reporting job (cloud/src/privacy.ts)

  1. When: tried at minute :37 every hour (Atlassian asks not to use the top of the hour), inside the normal cron run.
  2. What is due: every account id the app stores (signed-in users, and actors in history), whose last report is older than one cycle (7 days, or Cycle-Period). Each with the oldest time its data was fetched.
  3. Report: up to 3 requests of 90 accounts per run to POST /app/report-accounts/, using a valid access token (the app owner's if ATLASSIAN_APP_OWNER_ACCOUNT_ID is set).
  4. Act: closed → erase; updated → clear; record the report time for everyone else.
  5. Resume: progress lives in pdr_reports, so an interrupted cycle continues next hour. 429 → wait Retry-After; other errors → wait an hour.

First live report: 18 November 2026, 19:37 UTC, accepted by Atlassian.

Principles applied

  • Minimisation: store only what monitoring and history need.
  • Purpose limitation: data is used only to show and notify the same user.
  • Storage limitation: explicit retention for every table.
  • Transparency: the privacy policy (Cloud edition) describes all of this.
  • User control: disconnect, delete, revoke.
Local editionEverything stays on your computer: ~/.jira-bug-dashboard/config.json and jarvis.json. Delete the folder to remove all of it. No reporting applies, because no third party stores anything.
