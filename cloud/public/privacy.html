Privacy policy

Jira Bug Dashboard, Cloud edition · Last updated 18 November 2026

Jira Bug Dashboard is an open-source tool that shows the Jira bugs assigned to you and notifies you when they change. This page explains what the hosted Cloud edition at jira-bug-dashboard-cloud.shouryacode2021.workers.dev stores, why, and for how long. The source code is public at github.com/ShouryaSengar/jira-bug-dashboard, so you can check every statement below.

Who runs it

The service is operated by Shourya Singh Sengar as an individual open-source project. Contact: shouryacode2021@gmail.com.

What access the app asks for

When you sign in with Atlassian you grant these read-only permissions, for the Jira site you choose on Atlassian's consent screen:

  • read:jira-work: read issues, comments and change history you can already see in Jira
  • read:jira-user: read user names shown on those issues
  • read:me: your Atlassian name, e-mail and avatar, to identify your account
  • offline_access: lets the service keep checking for changes while you are away

The app cannot create, edit or delete anything in Jira. It only sees what your own Jira permissions allow.

Full Jira issue contents (descriptions, attachments, complete comments) are not stored. They are fetched live from Jira when you open them and passed straight to your browser.

Your browser also keeps a local copy of your settings and the last loaded results (in localStorage) so the page opens quickly. Signing out, disconnecting or deleting your account clears it. Tokens are never stored in the browser.

What is not done

  • No advertising, tracking or analytics scripts.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone except the services below, and only for the purpose stated.
  • No AI or machine-learning processing of your data. Notification wording comes from fixed templates.

Services involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and its database (Workers, D1; database region: Asia-Pacific). Cloudflare keeps short-lived request logs (URLs without sign-in codes, status codes, error messages) for operating the service.
  • Atlassian provides sign-in and the Jira data. See Atlassian's privacy policy.
  • Your browser's push service (Google for Chrome/Edge/Android, Mozilla for Firefox, Apple for Safari, Microsoft for Edge on Windows) relays notifications. Their content is end-to-end encrypted to your device, so the push service cannot read it.

Security

Tokens are encrypted at rest with a key stored outside the database, sign-in uses the OAuth state check, sessions use HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite cookies, and every database query is limited to the signed-in user. No system is perfectly secure; if you believe something is wrong, please e-mail the address above.

Your choices

  • Turn off notifications or monitoring any time in the JARVIS panel.
  • Disconnect Jira (Settings): deletes your tokens, site, monitoring data, devices and sessions immediately.
  • Delete account (Settings): permanently deletes everything stored about you.
  • Revoke access at Atlassian: id.atlassian.com → Connected apps.
  • For a copy of your data or any other request, e-mail the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated and the change is visible in the public source repository.
